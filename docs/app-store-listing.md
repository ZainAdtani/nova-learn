## Description

Nova Learn teaches you one science fact a day, in about 30 seconds.

Astronomy, chemistry, evolution, physics, and more, one bite-sized lesson at a time. Read a quick fact, answer one question, and keep your streak going.

**How it works**
- Open the app, read today's fact
- Answer one quick question about it
- Build your streak, one day at a time

**Built for people who are curious**
No badges to chase, no leaderboards, no clutter. Just a daily habit of learning something real.

**Your progress, wherever you go**
Sign in with just your email (no password needed) to save your streak permanently. Or skip sign-in entirely and use the app as a guest.
