Nova Learn Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

What we collect

If you use Nova Learn as a guest, we don't collect anything. Your streak is saved only on your own device.

If you choose to sign in, we collect:

  • Your email address, used only to sign you in (we send a one-time code, there's no password)
  • Your streak count and the date you joined, so your progress can follow you to a new phone

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data to anyone
  • We don't run ads or ad tracking
  • We don't collect your location, contacts, or photos
  • We don't share your email with any third party except our backend provider (Supabase), which stores your account and streak data securely on our behalf

How your data is stored

Signed-in account data is stored with Supabase, our backend provider. It is protected so that only you can see or change your own information.

Your choices

You can use the entire app without ever signing in. If you've signed in and want your account and data deleted, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
