Common questions

How do I save my streak?

Open Settings and tap "Save your streak". Enter your email and we'll send a 6-digit code, no password needed. Once you're signed in, your streak is saved to your account and follows you to a new phone.

How do I cancel my Premium subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, go to Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → Nova Learn Premium, then tap Cancel Subscription. You'll keep Premium until the end of the current billing period.

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open the 👑 Plus tab in the app and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us at zkadtani@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and we'll delete your account and data.

The app isn't working. What should I try?

  • Make sure you're on the latest version of the app from the App Store
  • Close the app fully and reopen it
  • Check your internet connection (sign-in and Premium need it)

If it's still not working, email us at zkadtani@gmail.com and tell us what happened and what iPhone model you have.
