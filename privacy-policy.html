NovaLearn Privacy Policy

Updated October 24, 2026. Applies to the NovaLearn Field Guide release, version 1.0.0.

The current app

NovaLearn does not require an account. We do not collect personal data, field notes, learning history or analytics from the current app. We do not run advertising or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your saved investigations, predictions, answers, optional field notes, appearance preference and learning streak are stored locally. They do not sync through a NovaLearn account. Device backups may contain app data according to your device settings. Removing the app may remove your local notes.

Reminders and purchases

Daily reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. We do not register a remote push token. Apple processes subscriptions and restoration through StoreKit. NovaLearn checks subscription access on the device. We do not run a server that receives your payment details.

Links and sharing

Science sources and legal pages open in your system browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies. If you choose to share a field note, the system share sheet sends only the text you select to your chosen destination. We do not receive a copy.

Accounts from earlier versions

Earlier versions offered optional email sign-in and stored an email address and streak with Supabase. The current version does not access those records. If you used an earlier account and want its records deleted, email zkadtani@gmail.com from that account address. Restoring the older database does not add account collection to the current app.
