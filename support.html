NovaLearn Support

Updated October 24, 2026. Applies to the NovaLearn Field Guide release, version 1.0.0.

NovaLearn is a pocket science field guide made by our team. For help, email za.backup1@gmail.com with your device model, app version and what happened.

Start an investigation

Open Today and choose Open today's investigation. Make a prediction, change the model, read the evidence, answer the application question and save a field note. Explore includes two open studies that are always free. The daily feature cycles through six investigations.

Where are my notes?

The Notebook tab shows saved investigations and notes. The current app has no sign-in or cloud sync. Notes stay on this device. Share an individual note from Notebook if you want a separate copy. Removing the app may remove local notes.

What works offline?

The investigations, models, notebook, search and appearance controls work without a network connection. Source pages, support pages, some sharing destinations and Apple purchases need a connection.

Manage Premium

Open Settings in NovaLearn, then NovaLearn Premium. Restore purchases uses the same Apple Account that purchased the subscription. To manage or cancel, choose Manage Apple subscriptions in Settings, or open device Settings, your Apple Account, Subscriptions. Removing the app does not cancel a subscription.

Reminders

Settings lets you enable or cancel one daily reminder at 9 AM in your local time. If notification permission is denied, allow NovaLearn notifications in device Settings first.

Earlier accounts

Earlier versions offered optional email sign-in. The current release does not use those accounts. For a deletion request about an earlier account, email us from the address associated with it.

Something is not working

Close and reopen the app. Check that you have the latest version. For store or source-page errors, check your internet connection. Contact us if the problem continues.
