You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your iPhone Settings: Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → Nova Learn Premium → Cancel Subscription. If you cancel, you keep Premium until the end of the current billing period. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

Restoring purchases

If you reinstall the app or switch devices, open the 👑 Plus tab and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.
