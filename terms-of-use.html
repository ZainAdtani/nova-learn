The field guide

NovaLearn is a general science learning app by Zain Adtani and Farid Virani. Volume 01 contains six complete investigations. The daily feature cycles through that collection. Today's investigation, two open studies and the notebook are free. Teaching diagrams are simplified illustrations, not scientific measurement tools.

Restore purchases

In NovaLearn, open Settings, NovaLearn Premium, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase. Restoration requires access to the App Store.
